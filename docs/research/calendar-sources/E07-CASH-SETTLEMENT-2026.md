# E07 research settlement calendar, October through December 2026

The reviewed interval is October 1 through December 31, inclusive. Regular-way
equity settlement uses T+1 and the first standard settlement business day
after a trade. In this interval the schedule is Monday through Friday,
excluding October 12, November 11, November 26 and December 25. The adapter
gets exchange-open days from a separate verified equity calendar.

- DTC notice 23036-25, published October 15, 2025, was retrieved November 22,
  2026 at 04:47:47.507128 UTC. It supplies the annual holiday schedule.
  October 12 and November 11 have some DTC services and no settlement.
  November 26 and December 25 are full DTC closures. The archived byte count is
  237,059 and the SHA-256 is
  `943e9bbb70de5ea0e074fa9942a6f08a5661a8a3f105c46326a5e8a29c39cddf`.
  [DTC notice](https://www.dtcc.com/-/media/Files/pdf/2025/10/15/23036-25.pdf)
- NSCC notice a9848, published October 27, 2026, was retrieved November 22,
  2026 at 04:47:47.766327 UTC. It confirms that equity markets would trade
  through the November 11 bank holiday and identifies an additional day of
  clearing exposure. The archived byte count is 171,367 and the SHA-256 is
  `bbb4dfec7877309a2d7267e8c3fe7d07fb3136f1f509df9d26a6d9185fee5d2f`.
  [NSCC notice](https://files.dtcc.com/download/assets/a9848/9a0314f6d24111f1afef42275106fa04)
- The U.S. regular-way standard moved to T+1 on May 28, 2024.
  [SEC T+1 guidance](https://www.sec.gov/exams/educationhelpguidesfaqs/t1-faq)

The observation clock is local file retrieval. Historical possession is
unverified. This projection assumes ordinary clearing service on unlisted
weekdays. A later verified closure or correction requires a new reviewed
version with a supersession reference. An extension requires supporting
sources through the last mapped cash-release date. A calendar date is a
model input for cash reuse. Broker credit time requires separate evidence.
